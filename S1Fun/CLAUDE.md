# S1Fun/

Function-space objects for functions on the circle (1-sphere) — the simplest of the three function-space hierarchies (see `SO3Fun/CLAUDE.md` for the shared abstract-interface pattern). `@S1Fun` is the abstract base; `@S1FunHandle` and `@S1FunHarmonic` (Fourier-series coefficients, `S1KernelFunctions/`) are the concrete representations. Used e.g. for angular profiles/sections through higher-dimensional ODF or pole-figure data rather than as a primary data type in its own right.
